# Pricing Strategy — Property Edge

## Tiers (SGD)

| Tier | Price/mo | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | $29 | HDB only, 50 reports/mo |
| Pro | $79 | HDB + URA private, unlimited reports, daily price-trend alerts |
| Team | $149 | 3 seats, custom branding, priority support |

## First-month strategy

- **Free 30-day trial for first 20 agents** in exchange for testimonial + feedback
- After 20 testimonials, drop free trial to 7 days
- **Annual prepay**: 2 months free (e.g. Pro = $790/yr instead of $948)

## Why these numbers

- **PropertyGuru AgentNet**: ~SGD 200/mo basic — we're 1/3 the price
- **99 Pro subscription**: ~SGD 138/mo — we're under
- **REDX-equivalent SG product**: doesn't really exist — positioning room
- Singapore agents earn SGD 5k–30k/mo — $29 is impulse-buy territory

## Math to SGD 6,000/mo (~ USD 150/day)

| Mix | Customers | MRR (SGD) |
|---|---|---|
| All Starter | 200 | 5,800 |
| All Pro | 75 | 5,925 |
| All Team | 40 | 5,960 |
| **Realistic blend** | 50 Starter + 50 Pro + 5 Team | **6,145** |

## Upgrade path

- Start every customer on Starter (lowest friction)
- After 30 days: prompt them to upgrade if they hit the report cap or ask about private property
- After 60 days: pitch Team tier to anyone whose agency has 2+ subscribers

## Churn assumptions

- Monthly churn: ~10% (B2B SaaS at this price point)
- To net +10 customers/month, need to acquire ~15
- After year 1, churn should drop to 5% as product matures
