# LinkedIn + Telegram Outreach Kit — Singapore Property Agents

## Where to find agents

- **CEA Public Register** — https://www.cea.gov.sg/aceas/public/svc1101 — every licensed Singapore agent + their agency
- **LinkedIn search** — `"property agent" Singapore` filtered to "posted in last 30 days" (active agents)
- **Telegram property groups** — search for "Singapore property", "SG real estate agents" on Telegram
- **PropertyGuru / 99.co listings** — agents are named on every listing; high-volume listers are who you want
- **Facebook groups** — "Singapore Property Agents Network", "PropNex / ERA / Huttons community" groups

## Cold message template — LinkedIn

> Hi [Name], saw your listings around [district/area] — nice work on [recent listing or transaction].
>
> Quick one: I built a comp-sales tool that pulls URA caveats + HDB resale for any address in 30 seconds. Trying it with a few [area] agents this month before opening it wider.
>
> Want a free month? No card, just feedback. I'll send a sample [area] report so you can see the output.

## Follow-up (3 days, no reply)

> Hey [Name], no pressure — happy to just send the sample report so you can decide. Takes me 2 minutes to pull.

## If they say yes

> Great. What's a recent listing or area you're working on? I'll pull the comp report so you see exactly what you'd get from a paid subscription.

(Then send the actual CSV/PDF generated by the tool. Quality of this single report wins or loses the deal.)

## Telegram property group strategy

**Don't pitch directly** — most groups ban it instantly. Instead:

1. Be helpful for 1–2 weeks: answer questions, share market insights, no link drops.
2. When someone asks "what's the going rate for X?" or "any recent comps in Y?" → reply with a screenshot from your tool, plus a one-line note on the trend.
3. People will DM asking how you pulled it. Convert in DM, not in group.

## What NOT to do (Singapore-specific)

- ❌ Mass DMs (LinkedIn flags, CEA frowns on it)
- ❌ Buying CEA contact lists (PDPA violation, fines)
- ❌ Cold-calling agents (DNC registry + low ROI)
- ❌ Scraping personal contact info from PropertyGuru/99 (TOS + PDPA)
- ❌ Reselling agent contact data (PDPA — you'd need their consent)

## Realistic outreach numbers

- Send rate: 20–30 personalised messages/day on LinkedIn (any more = flagged)
- Reply rate (good message + relevant timing): 15–25%
- Trial-to-paid conversion: 20–30%
- Math: 25 messages/day × 20% reply × 25% convert = ~1.25 paid customers/day at the top of funnel

To hit 100 customers (≈ SGD 6,000 MRR) → roughly 80 outreach days. Realistic 3–4 month timeline.
